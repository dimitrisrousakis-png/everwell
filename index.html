Challenge scope

A 30-day plank and push-up challenge built around consistency, core strength, upper-body strength, recovery, and participation.

How it works

Each active participant follows one of three progressive programs over 30 days. Every day includes one plank exercise, a recovery day, or a short circuit. Participants mark each day complete on their individual page, and progress is saved automatically in the browser.

Progression

Alternate between forearm plank and side plank sessions, add progressive push-ups, and use planned recovery days. Forearm plank builds from 60 to 120 seconds; side plank remains 30 seconds per side; push-ups build from 10 to 20.

Challenge goals

Improve core endurance, body control, posture, shoulder stability, and consistency. The challenge is not a competition based on absolute hold time. Team participation reflects completed days, so every program is treated fairly.

Safety

Good form is more important than duration. Stop an exercise if you feel sharp pain, numbness, dizziness, or unusual discomfort. Longer holds may be divided into shorter sets when form begins to break down.
